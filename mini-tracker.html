Base Ring Templates

Default outer size (25.4mm = 1 inch) matches a standard battle-map square exactly. The default 20mm inner cutout leaves a ~2.7mm ring — workable for most standard mini bases, but check it against your own bases before printing a whole set; a narrower ring means smaller, harder-to-read markers.
When you print: set your printer's scale to "100%" / "Actual size" (not "Fit to page") — these are sized in real millimeters, and page-fit scaling will throw that off.
